Nifty in 60

Roz shaam, 60 seconds. Sirf data, salaah nahi.

Nifty in 60 is a YouTube channel that posts a one-minute summary of Indian market data every trading evening: Nifty 50 and Nifty Bank closing levels, pivot support and resistance, nearby open interest, and FII/DII figures. All data comes from NSE India's public end-of-day files.

"Nifty in 60 Uploader" is the small program that builds each video and uploads it to the channel. It is used only by the channel's owner, for this one channel.

The channel is for education and information only. Nothing on it is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
