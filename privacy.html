Privacy policy: Nifty in 60 Uploader

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What this app is

Nifty in 60 Uploader is a private tool that uploads videos to the Nifty in 60 YouTube channel. It is operated by the channel's owner and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own channel, read that channel's list of videos, and edit or remove videos on that channel. It does not access any other Google account, channel or user.

What it collects and stores

The app collects no personal information about viewers or anyone else. It stores only the access credentials needed to upload to the owner's channel, kept as encrypted secrets. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are sent to YouTube for publishing; YouTube's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the channel's page: youtube.com/@NiftyIn60.
